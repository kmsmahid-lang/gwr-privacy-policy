Privacy Policy

GWR Tour - Free Fire Tournament Platform
Last Updated: 07 October 2026

Welcome to GWR Tour ("we", "our", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and website (the "Service"). Please read this policy carefully.

By using GWR Tour, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

When you register for an account, we collect:

  • Username (your in-game name)
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Encrypted password
  • Device ID (for security and fraud prevention)

1.2 Payment Information

For deposits and withdrawals, we collect:

  • Mobile banking number (bKash / Nagad / Rocket)
  • Transaction ID (TrxID)
  • Deposit/withdrawal amount
  • Timestamp of transaction

⚠️ We do NOT store your bKash/Nagad/Rocket PIN, password, or OTP. We only verify the transaction using the TrxID sent to our merchant account.

1.3 SMS Permission

Our app may request SMS reading permission (READ_SMS, RECEIVE_SMS) solely to automatically verify deposit transactions sent from bKash/Nagad/Rocket to our merchant number.

  • We do NOT read your personal SMS.
  • We do NOT upload your SMS to any server.
  • We only detect payment SMS from bKash/Nagad/Rocket.
  • All SMS processing is done locally on your device.

1.4 Device Information

  • Device model, operating system, and browser type
  • IP address and approximate location
  • App usage statistics
  • Unique device identifier (for fraud prevention)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Account Management: To create and manage your account
  • Tournament Participation: To register you in matches and distribute prizes
  • Payment Verification: To verify deposits and process withdrawals
  • Security: To prevent fraud, cheating, and abuse
  • Communication: To send important notifications and updates
  • Improvement: To analyze usage patterns and improve the Service

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do NOT sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We may share data only in the following cases:

  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or court order
  • Fraud Prevention: With security partners to prevent fraud
  • Service Providers: With Firebase (Google) for authentication and database services
  • Business Transfer: In case of merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • All data transmission uses HTTPS encryption
  • Passwords are hashed and salted
  • Firebase Realtime Database with strict security rules
  • Device ban system to prevent unauthorized access
  • Regular security audits

🔒 However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. After account deletion:

  • Personal data is deleted within 30 days
  • Transaction records may be kept for 90 days for legal/accounting purposes
  • Anonymized statistics may be retained indefinitely

6. Children's Privacy

Our Service is NOT intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If we discover such data, we will delete it immediately. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

  • Access: Request a copy of your data
  • Correction: Update incorrect information
  • Deletion: Request account and data deletion
  • Portability: Receive your data in a portable format
  • Objection: Object to certain data processing

To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.
